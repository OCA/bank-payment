Glue between ``account_payment_schedule`` and
``account_payment_order_api``, installed automatically with both.

- An item whose payment is refused, rejected, cancelled or returned by
  the bank comes back to the payment schedule right away, even while the
  rest of its order is still at the bank.
- The schedule shows the status and the reason given by the bank to the
  last payment of each item.
- Three counters on top of the schedule: awaiting release at the bank,
  refused by the bank (items back in the schedule) and outcome unknown.
