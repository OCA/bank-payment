This module adds a payment schedule: open vendor bills grouped by the
date on which they should be paid.

Each item gets a scheduled payment date, which starts as the due date,
and an instrument: the payment mode and, when the payment method needs
one, the partner bank account. Items that are ready are put in draft
payment orders, one per payment mode and date, with that fixed date.
Confirming the order is the approval to pay, so
``account_payment_order_tier_validation`` adds approval rules without
anything else.

No new document is created: the schedule is a view over the journal
items and their draft payment orders. An item that is in a draft,
confirmed or generated order cannot be scheduled again, the same rule as
the wizard of the payment order.

On top of the schedule, a cash flow chart for the next 30 days shows the
projected balance per day: the balance of the bank and cash journals
today, plus receivables by due date, minus the scheduled payments.
Overdue receivables are shown apart and left out of the balance. The
dates below the minimum balance of the company are flagged, and the
wizard and the draft payment order warn about them without blocking.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Invoicing > Vendors > Payment Schedule*. Open items are
   grouped by scheduled payment date. The counters on top show what is
   to schedule, due until today, overdue, missing an instrument, in a
   draft order or in progress; a click shows only those items, a second
   click shows all open items again.
2. Change the scheduled date, the payment mode or the partner bank
   account in the list. Several items can be edited at once.
3. Select the items and click *Create Payment Orders*. The wizard shows
   what goes into the orders and what is left out, with the reason. A
   date in the past becomes today. A bill blocked for payment (*Block*
   button on the bill) is left out.
4. Confirm each payment order as usual. Changing the date of an item
   that is in a draft order moves it to the order of the new date;
   *Remove from Order* puts it back in the schedule.
5. Set the minimum balance in *Invoicing > Configuration > Settings*,
   section *Vendor Payments*. The *Short of Cash* counter and filter
   show the items scheduled on a date whose projected balance is below
   it.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Schedule of customer items (direct debit) from the receivables menu.
- Items refused by the bank in ``account_payment_order_api`` should come
  back to the schedule; this needs a small glue module.
- Payment priority and a button to move the lower priority items to the
  first date with enough balance.
