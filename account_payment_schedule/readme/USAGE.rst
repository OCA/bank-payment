#. Go to *Invoicing > Vendors > Payment Schedule*. Open items are grouped by
   scheduled payment date. The counters on top show what is to schedule, due
   until today, overdue, missing an instrument, in a draft order or in progress;
   a click shows only those items, a second click shows all open items again.
#. Change the scheduled date, the payment mode or the partner bank account in the
   list. Several items can be edited at once.
#. Select the items and click *Create Payment Orders*. The wizard shows what goes
   into the orders and what is left out, with the reason. A date in the past
   becomes today.
#. Confirm each payment order as usual. Changing the date of an item that is in a
   draft order moves it to the order of the new date; *Remove from Order* puts it
   back in the schedule.
#. Set the minimum balance in *Invoicing > Configuration > Settings*, section
   *Payment Orders*. The *Short of Cash* counter and filter show the items
   scheduled on a date whose projected balance is below it.
