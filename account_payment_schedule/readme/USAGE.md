1. Go to *Invoicing > Vendors > Payment Schedule*. Open items are grouped by
   scheduled payment date. The counters on top show what is to schedule, due
   until today, overdue, missing an instrument, in a draft order or in progress;
   a click shows only those items, a second click shows all open items again.
2. Change the scheduled date, the payment mode or the partner bank account in the
   list. Several items can be edited at once.
3. Select the items and click *Create Payment Orders*. The wizard shows what goes
   into the orders and what is left out, with the reason. A date in the past
   becomes today. A bill blocked for payment (*Block* button on the bill) is left
   out.
4. Confirm each payment order as usual. Changing the date of an item that is in a
   draft order moves it to the order of the new date; *Remove from Order* puts it
   back in the schedule.
5. Set the minimum balance in *Invoicing > Configuration > Settings*, section
   *Vendor Payments*. The *Short of Cash* counter and filter show the items
   scheduled on a date whose projected balance is below it.
