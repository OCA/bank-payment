#. Set the *Payment Priority* of the vendors in the *Sales & Purchase* tab of the
   partner. New bills take it as the priority of their items.
#. In *Invoicing > Vendors > Payment Schedule*, change the priority of an item in
   the *Payment Priority* column, or use the priority filters and grouping.
#. Click *Replan by Priority* above the cash flow chart. Check the preview and
   click *Apply*: the new dates are written on the items, and the items of draft
   payment orders move to the order of their new date.
