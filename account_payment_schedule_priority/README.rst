When cash is short, this module decides who gets paid first in the
payment schedule of ``account_payment_schedule``.

Each journal item gets a payment priority: Critical, High, Normal or
Low. It comes from the partner and can be changed on the item in the
schedule, one at a time or several at once. The schedule can be filtered
and grouped by priority.

*Replan by Priority*, on top of the cash flow chart, looks at the dates
of the next 30 days whose projected balance is below the minimum
balance. On each of them, the lowest priority items, latest due date
first, move to the first date from which the projected balance stays at
or above the minimum, until the date is no longer short. Critical items
and items of confirmed payment orders keep their date. A preview lists
the items to move, the items kept because no later date has enough
balance, and the dates that stay short; nothing changes before it is
applied.

Usage
=====

1. Set the *Payment Priority* of the vendors in the *Sales & Purchase*
   tab of the partner. New bills take it as the priority of their items.
2. In *Invoicing > Vendors > Payment Schedule*, change the priority of
   an item in the *Payment Priority* column, or use the priority filters
   and grouping.
3. Click *Replan by Priority* above the cash flow chart. Check the
   preview and click *Apply*: the new dates are written on the items,
   and the items of draft payment orders move to the order of their new
   date.
