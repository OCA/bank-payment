This module targets the **native early payment discount of Odoo 18**
(payment terms with *Early Discount*, discount percentage and discount
days). Without it, ``account_payment_order`` (SEPA credit transfer and
others) is not usable for vendor bills with such payment terms:

- the *Create Transactions* wizard only selects bills by their net due
  date, i.e. after the discount period has ended;
- the payment line always takes the full amount, the discount is never
  deducted;
- a manually reduced amount leaves the bill partially open, because the
  payment entry of the order does not book the discount (unlike the
  *Register Payment* wizard).

This module makes payment orders behave like *Register Payment* for
these payment terms:

- the *Create Transactions* wizard can also select bills whose discount
  must be paid up to the filter date, not only bills that are due by
  then;
- payment lines get the **discounted amount** and, if the payment mode
  pays on the discount date, the **discount date** as execution date
  (moved to a working day); this amount and date go into the payment
  file;
- when the order is confirmed, the lines follow the current open amount
  of the bill (e.g. a credit note reconciled in the meantime) and the
  discount is checked again against the actual execution date (full
  amount and a chatter note if the discount period is over; manually
  changed amounts are kept). A transfer requested on a non-working day
  is executed by the bank on the next working day, the discount is only
  kept if that day is still within the discount period;
- before the payment file is generated, the lines are checked again: if
  a bill was settled in the meantime or its discount period is over at
  the execution date (the date of the line, or today if it is past), the
  order must be confirmed again;
- when the payments are posted (on upload, or a payment of the order
  posted on its own), the payment entry books the discount and the tax
  correction exactly like the *Register Payment* wizard, so the bill is
  fully reconciled.

Credit notes and partial settlements, which Odoo does not handle for
early payment discounts (no discount once a bill is partially
reconciled, credit notes deducted in full):

- open discount = discount of the bill minus the discount of what is
  already settled: a credit note showing a discount takes away its own
  discount, one without discount nothing, a partial payment the
  proportional discount;
- a credit note in the same payment is deducted with its discounted
  amount only; its discount is booked back on the discount account with
  the tax correction, using the taxes, tax grids and accounts of the
  credit note (fixed taxes are not discounted, as in Odoo). A credit
  note with a discount settled before is booked back the same way, so
  each document corrects its own taxes;
- in orders paying on the due date, a credit note is deducted from the
  first payment taking a discount for the same partner, instead of being
  a (negative) payment of its own on its due date.

Direct debit orders handle customer invoices with an early discount the
same way: an amount collected within the discount period is collected
with the discount, as the customer would get it with *Register Payment*.
Collecting on the net due date (option *Pay Early Discounts on Discount
Date* disabled) collects the full amount.

Configuration
=============

1. In *Invoicing > Configuration > Settings*, set the *Early Discount
   Gain* and *Early Discount Loss* accounts (used by Odoo for the native
   early payment discount).
2. Give vendors payment terms with *Early Discount* (percentage, days
   and the tax computation of the discount).
3. On the payment modes used in payment orders (*Invoicing >
   Configuration > Payment Modes*), section *Early Payment Discount*:

   - *Pay Early Discounts on Discount Date*: bills with a discount are
     selected and scheduled by their discount date instead of their due
     date;
   - *Extend Discount Period to Next Working Day*: a discount period
     ending on a non-working day (weekend or public holiday of the
     company's working-time calendar) is extended to the next working
     day;
   - *Early Discount Buffer Days*: bills are selected and scheduled that
     many days before the end of the discount period (on the working day
     before), so that the money reaches the supplier in time, e.g. 1 to
     transfer one day early. Count the days the transfer needs to be
     credited to the supplier, which can be more for cross-border or
     non-SEPA payments. The discount itself stays valid until the end of
     the discount period;
   - *Early Discount Grace Days*: additional days after the end of the
     discount period during which the discount is still deducted,
     relying on the supplier's goodwill.

Usage
=====

1. Create a payment order and click *Create Transactions*.
2. Set the filter date and check *Include Early Discounts*: bills whose
   discount must be paid by the filter date (end of the discount period
   minus the buffer days) are proposed in addition to the due bills. For
   orders paying on the due date, this needs the option *Pay Early
   Discounts on Discount Date* of the payment mode.
3. The payment lines show the discount date and the deducted discount;
   the amount is the discounted amount.
4. Confirm the order: lines whose discount period is over at the
   execution date go back to the full amount, with a note in the
   chatter.
5. Generate the payment file: if a bill was settled in the meantime or
   its discount period is over, set the order back to draft and confirm
   it again.
6. Upload the payment file: the payment entries book the discount and
   the bills are fully reconciled.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The discount is only deducted when the payment line is in the currency
  of the bill (the default when the line is created from the bill). A
  line changed to another currency is paid without discount.
