1. Create a payment order and click _Create Transactions_.
2. Set the filter date and check _Include Early Discounts_: bills whose discount must be
   paid by the filter date (end of the discount period minus the buffer days) are
   proposed in addition to the due bills. For orders paying on the due date, this needs
   the option _Pay Early Discounts on Discount Date_ of the payment mode.
3. The payment lines show the discount date and the deducted discount; the amount is the
   discounted amount.
4. Confirm the order: lines whose discount period is over at the execution date go back
   to the full amount, with a note in the chatter.
5. Generate the payment file: if a bill was settled in the meantime or its discount
   period is over, set the order back to draft and confirm it again.
6. Upload the payment file: the payment entries book the discount and the bills are
   fully reconciled.
